How Panig reads a story

The editorial unit is the story, not a permanent brand on a newsroom or creator. AI prepares the comparison. Humans review labels that could harm reputation or overstate certainty. Reach is an influence signal — never a truth signal.

  1. Collect public reporting, creator posts, and primary records from a deliberately mixed watchlist.
  2. Cluster items that refer to the same underlying event, keeping each version intact.
  3. Score signals against quoted spans: evidence, headline fit, loaded language, certainty, framing, omissions, tone.
  4. Publish the comparison with original links, uncertainty, and a corrections log.

Labels such as “demonstrably false” or coordination claims require documentary evidence and human approval. Unsupported claims do not receive equal weight with authenticated records.

Panig is Filipino for side. This prototype shows how a day’s news can be read as competing panig — newsroom, influencer, and primary evidence — without asking you to trust only our summary.
